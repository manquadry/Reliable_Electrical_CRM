Role: Executive Admin

3. Executive Management (Admin) Workflows

The Managing Director has unrestricted governance across commercial metrics, field operations, and accounting.

1
Executive Dashboard Overview (/dashboard)
Inspect real-time revenue attainment vs target (₦), total active field executives, registered commercial stores, and pending order dispatches. Review territory distribution charts and the live activity stream.
2
Date-by-Date Daily Activity Tracker (/activities/daily)
Select any specific date on the interactive calendar picker. The view summarizes:
  • Daily tally: Total store visits, orders booked, collections logged, new retailers onboarded.
  • Agent Breakdown: Individual cards for Chinedu, Fatima, Emeka, and Tunde.
  • Time-stamped audit logs with visit notes and clickable GPS coordinates linking directly to Google Maps.
3
Field Sales Executive Onboarding (/agents/create)
Register new field agents into the CRM. Set official email, unique Employee Code (REL-FE-XXX), assigned sales territory, monthly sales quota (₦), daily visit benchmark, monthly store onboarding quota, and allocated company vehicle (Motorbike/Van/Car).
4
Customer Onboarding Audit & Directory (/customers)
Audit all electrical retail stores and building contractors onboarded by field agents. Verify exact onboarding dates, originating field agent, assigned credit terms, and physical GPS storefront coordinates.
5
Activity Audit & One-Click CSV Export (/activities/report)
Filter activities by agent, activity category, and custom date range. Click Export CSV to immediately generate an audit spreadsheet for board meetings, transport allowances, and commission calculations.
6
Sales Orders & Formal Printable Waybills (/orders)
Manage order statuses (Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered). Open any order to generate a branded, printable A4 Delivery Waybill & Invoice from Gbagada central warehouse with signature sign-off lines.
Key Management Decision Point: The Date-by-Date Daily Tracker solves field opacity. Executive management can verify whether a field executive was physically present at Alaba or Ikeja at the reported time via the GPS link and corresponding order booking timestamp.

4. Field Sales Executive Workflows

Field agents use mobile smartphones and tablets on the road to register storefronts, check in, and close deals.

1
Agent Field Dashboard & Mobile Navigation Bar
When logged in as a field agent, the interface personalizes to their territory, monthly quota attainment bar, today's visit target counter, and mobile-optimized sticky bottom navigation bar (Home, Daily Log, Check-In, Customers, Orders).
2
Mobile GPS Check-In (/activities/check-in)
During a physical client visit, the agent taps Check-In:
  • Selects existing customer or enters prospective client name.
  • Selects visit purpose (Routine Restock, Pitch, Order Taking, Debt Collection, Complaint).
  • Taps Capture Current Location (GPS): Browser automatically stamps decimal coordinates.
  • Enters visit notes & outcomes, then submits. Instantly updates the Admin Daily Tracker.
3
Customer Onboarding in the Field (/customers/create)
When discovering a new electrical dealer or contractor, the agent completes the onboarding form:
  • Business Name, Owner/Contact Name, Phone, Email, Physical Address, and Landmark.
  • Category: Wholesaler, Lighting Gallery, Retailer, Electrical Contractor.
  • Captures store GPS location, sets agreed Credit Limit (₦) and Payment Terms (e.g. Net 14 Days).
  • Automatically stamps onboarding date and links the client to the agent's account.
4
Instant Sales Order Booking (/orders/create)
Select customer and pick items from the catalog (LED floodlights, distribution boards, MCBs, chandeliers, copper cables). Quantities, wholesale discount tiers, and totals calculate live. Submit order to initiate warehouse dispatch.
5
Logging Payment Collections (/payments/create)
Record bank transfers, POS receipts, cheques, or cash collected from customers against active orders. Enter transaction reference to instantly credit customer balance.
Zero App Installation Required: Built as a high-performance web application accessible on any Android or iOS browser with native geolocation support, low data consumption, and fast load times.

  • Print-optimized CSS rules (@media print)
